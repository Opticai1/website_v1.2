YAPPING


hello, I am Yufei!

You can probably relate to me as I used to try to find my passion by experimenting with many different things during middle school until high school.
I am a former GFX artist and 3D modeler for mostly Roblox games back then from 2020 until 2024. The tools I used were mostly Photoshop and Blender. I started out by making some casual GFX for fun and selling some of my work to my friends for a small amount of money. Not long after, I started to gain traction through joining GFX communities on Discord and Twitter (X) and started to get more and more clients. Things did work out pretty well and I made a decent earning while gaining more fame in the Roblox community and experience especially through advertising on Twitter (X) or Discord. But eventually, as all things have a beginning and an end, I became burnt out during times because I was neglecting some school work in order to focus on making money. Soon enough, I became totally burnt out for graphic design and decided to take a break. And um... I never really came back to making GFX because I put more time and effort into school. I still look back at my own graphic designs from time to time and wonder how I had the time and patience to make all of that, like I'm totally blown away by my own work. Honestly, I believe those 4 years weren't wasted at all and I think I did find one of my passions in life.

With my experience in graphic design, I began exploring other areas, such as web development, as I figured that my skills would be useful in designing websites. I didn't really start to do some programming stuff until I signed up for a 1 week course for html and css at a 42 coding school on-campus in 2024. Some time later, in 2025/2025, I grew an interest for Linux servers and this whole networking stuff. At that time I was passionate about hosting stuff and getting into this whole homelabbing rabbithole thing. I even hosted a Minecraft server for multiple friends to play on for a couple of months. So now in 2026 I am seriously beginning to learn web development and I guess one way to do this is to to just buy a domain and host a website on it.
