hello, I am Yufei!

You can probably relate to me, as I used to try to find my passion by experimenting with many different things during middle school until high school.
I am a former GFX artist and 3D modeler for mostly Roblox games back in 2020 until 2024. The essential tools I used were Photoshop and Blender. Like almost everyone, I started out by making some GFX for fun and selling some of my work to my friends for a small amount of money. Not long after, I started to gain traction through joining GFX communities on Discord and X and started to get more and more clients. Things did work out pretty well and I made a decent earning while gaining more popularity in the Roblox community and experience especially through advertisements on X or Discord. A few years pass and I became totally burnt out for graphic design and decided to take a break and put more focus on my studies.
As for 3D modelling, I know how to make low poly models and doing some basic texturing in both Blender and Substance Painter. I did make some sales on the side but it wasn't really my main focus. Nevertheless, I felt like I had a lot of fun during these years and learned some more valuable skills.

Lately, I began exploring other skills in the programming field and I figured that my skills would be useful in frontend development (which is why I made this website for testing). I didn't really start code until I signed up for a 1 week course for HTML and CSS at a 42 coding school on-campus in 2024. Near the end of 2025 I developed an interest for Linux servers and networking. During that time I hosted various applications on my homelab like cloud services or game servers. And that's how I learned a lot about server management and networking.

Now I'm just starting to pursue programming out of fun and curiosity which is why I'm studying CS at the moment.

Feel free to check out my past work!
